## 🔥 Stop Getting Fooled by Confident AI Nonsense

You asked AI a question. It answered smoothly, confidently — and it was completely wrong.

AI has a confidence problem. It speaks with total certainty about things that are outdated, hallucinated, or just wrong.

**This framework is a structured self-verification system for AI search.** Not a better prompt — a complete audit before AI opens its mouth.

---

## ⚡ The Three Core Rules

```
1. Mode Tiers       →  How serious = How thorough
2. Confidence Labels →  Every conclusion labeled High/Medium/Low/Extremely Low
3. Falsification    →  AI must search "why wrong" not just "why right"
```

---

## 🎯 Who's This For

- You use AI for research and decisions
- You rely on AI for investment, legal, or career advice
- You build AI products and want reliable outputs
- You're done being misled by confident hallucinations

## 🧠 Quick Reference

**Three Modes:**
| Mode | Use When | Search Rounds |
|------|----------|---------------|
| Quick | Facts, low-risk | 1 |
| Standard | Decisions, risk | 2 + falsification |
| Deep | Investment, legal, life-altering | 4+ |

**One Signal to Remember:**
> AI answers too fast, too smooth, too confidently = **danger signal**

---

## 📋 13 Taboos

Every AI that touches this framework must not:
1. Force conclusions without info
2. Hide sources
3. Only show supporting evidence
4. Check bias as a formality
5. Use vague freshness labels
6. Give vague next steps
7. Fit evidence to a preset answer
8. Use combined confidence labels ("medium-high")
9. Output meta-analysis / self-evaluation / summary
10. Skip information organization for long questions
11. Skip counter-evidence search
12. Pass impressions as verified facts
13. Cite numbers without sources

---

## 🌐 Background

The quality of AI search is not determined by the AI. It's determined by the framework you give the AI.

This project distills expert methodology on "how to judge if an AI answer is trustworthy" into a structured, executable process.

Built for personal use, open-sourced for everyone.

# AI搜索框架 / AI Search Framework

## 🔥 你是否经历过这些？

- 问 AI 一个问题，它答得头头是道，你信了，结果是错的
- AI 给了一个"2024年的数据"，你用了才发现那是训练数据，早过期了
- 同一个问题问三遍，AI 给三个不同答案，你不知道该信哪个
- 网上搜到一篇营销稿，AI 当真理引用，你还不知道

**这套框架是给 AI 用的思维校验系统。** 不是更好的提示词，而是让 AI 在回答之前，先完成一套完整的自我审查。

---

## ⚡ 三条核心规则

```
1. 模式分级     →  问题越重大，审查越严格
2. 置信度标注   →  每个结论必须标注 高/中/低/极低
3. 证伪搜索     →  AI必须搜"为什么可能错"，而不是只搜"为什么对"
```

---

## 🎯 适用人群

- 经常用 AI 查资料、做决策的人
- 需要 AI 提供投资、法律、职业建议参考的人
- AI 应用开发者，想让 AI 输出更可靠的人
- 不想被 AI 幻觉误导的任何人

## 🧠 快速参考

**三种模式：**
| 模式 | 何时用 | 搜索轮数 |
|------|--------|---------|
| 快速 | 低风险琐事、事实查询 | 1轮 |
| 标准 | 分析决策、风险评估 | 2轮（含证伪） |
| 深度 | 投资、法律、重大方向 | 4轮以上 |

**一个记住的态度：**
> AI 答得太快、太自信、太顺滑 = **危险信号**

---

## 📋 13条禁忌

使用本框架时，禁止：
1. 信息不足强行下结论
2. 隐瞒来源和存疑点
3. 只呈现支持证据
4. 偏差自检只写形式
5. 时效标注模糊
6. 行动建议模糊、不可执行
7. 配合预设答案
8. 置信度用组合词
9. 输出元分析/自我评价/总结
10. 长问题不梳理直接分析
11. 反对观点换词搜索跳过
12. 把印象伪装成搜过
13. 具体数字没搜过来源

---

**设计理念：** AI 搜索的质量不是由 AI 本身决定，而是由提问者提供的思维框架决定。
